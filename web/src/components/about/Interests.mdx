### Architecture, Civil Engineering, and Urban Planning

I regularly dream about buildings and urban spaces.

#### Skyscrapers

In a [501 Queen streetcar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/501_Queen) during a school field trip, a chaperone spotted me gazing at [Scotia Plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotia_Plaza) and introduced me to the word "[skyscraper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscraper)." While I insist we do not build tall buildings everywhere, I am generally a fan, and the [Skyscraper Museum](https://skyscraper.org/) is my favorite museum in New York City.
